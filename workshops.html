AI Video Production

Make video with AI that still feels directed.

AI can give you a shot in minutes. Making it mean something still takes an idea and a director. I show how I take a piece from brief to final frame with AI tools, and how to keep the look, the characters and the story consistent from shot to shot. We also cover where AI fits, where it doesn’t, and how to be open with clients about it.

You will learn

  1. Turning a brief into an idea and a shot list
  2. Building a consistent look: style frames, characters, places
  3. Directing image and video models shot by shot
  4. Choosing, fixing and upscaling what the tools give you
  5. Edit, sound and grade to finish the piece

For

Creatives, motion designers, editors and marketers who want to make video with AI without lowering the bar.

You leave with

A short piece made with AI, and a workflow you can repeat.

Creative Directing

Find the idea, make the decisions, lead the work to the last frame.

Creative direction is a series of decisions: why a piece exists, how it should feel, and the one choice that makes it work. I started as a VFX artist and grew into the role, so this workshop stays practical. We start from a brief, find the idea, write the treatment, then direct it through production and the edit.

You will learn

  1. Reading a brief and finding the idea behind it
  2. Writing a treatment and building references that sell it
  3. Setting the look: frame, colour, type and sound
  4. Giving direction and feedback that moves the work
  5. Presenting the idea and holding it in the room

For

Designers, motion artists, art directors and filmmakers stepping into creative direction.

You leave with

A treatment and a short piece you directed, from brief to final cut.

Motion Design

Timing, type and craft that hold up frame by frame.

Good motion starts before the first keyframe. I show how I design frames that work as stills, then give them timing, rhythm and weight. We go from style frames to a finished animation, with the habits from broadcast that keep work clean and on time.

You will learn

  1. Style frames and boards that carry the idea
  2. Timing, spacing and easing that feel right
  3. Kinetic type and transitions
  4. Moving between 2D and 3D
  5. Scenes built to change fast, and clean delivery

For

Designers starting in motion, and motion designers who want sharper craft.

You leave with

A finished animation for your reel, and a project setup you can reuse.
