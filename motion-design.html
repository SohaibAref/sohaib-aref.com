About

Good motion starts before the first keyframe.

I show how I design frames that work as stills, then give them timing, rhythm and weight. We go from style frames to a finished animation, with the habits from broadcast television that keep work clean and on time.

You will learn

  1. Frames firstStyle frames and boards that carry the idea before anything moves.
  2. TimingSpacing and easing that give movement weight and intent.
  3. Type in motionKinetic type and transitions that read clearly at speed.
  4. 2D and 3DMoving between them in one piece without the seams showing.
  5. Build and deliverScenes you can change fast, and clean files when it ships.

The fit

Who it’s for

Designers starting in motion, and motion designers who want sharper craft.

You leave with

A finished animation for your reel, and a project setup you can reuse.
