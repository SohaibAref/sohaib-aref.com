About

AI can give you a shot in minutes. Making it mean something still takes an idea and a director.

In this workshop I show how I take a piece from brief to final frame with AI tools, and how to keep the look, the characters and the story consistent from shot to shot. We also cover where AI fits, where it doesn’t, and how to be open with clients about it.

You will learn

  1. From brief to shot listTurn a brief into one clear idea, then into the shots that tell it.
  2. A consistent lookStyle frames, characters and places that stay the same from shot to shot.
  3. Directing the modelsPrompting image and video tools shot by shot, the way you would direct a crew.
  4. Choose, fix, upscalePicking the takes that work and repairing the ones that almost do.
  5. FinishingEdit, sound and grade, so the piece holds together as a film.

From the storyboard

Every film starts as a storyboard: the idea, the shots and their order, decided before anything is generated.

The fit

Who it’s for

Creatives, motion designers, editors and marketers who want to make video with AI without lowering the bar.

You leave with

A short piece made with AI, and a workflow you can repeat.
